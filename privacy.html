AirKickers privacy policy · prepared 15 October 2026

AirKickers is developed by Malkiel Shaul (ApptunesLabs). You can play without creating an account. This policy describes the intended iOS service configuration; paid subscriptions and live advertising remain disabled until release checks are complete.

What stays on your device

Your scores, training progress, challenges, medals, earned coins, cosmetic purchases and preferences are stored locally. These are not a cloud backup. Removing the app may remove them. We do not request your name, email, exact birth date, contacts, camera, microphone or precise location to play.

Age choices and children

Before optional services start, you can choose Under 18, 18 or older, or Prefer not to say. No adult option is selected by default. The choice stays on your device. Under 18 and undisclosed ages receive the full game without ad requests or optional Firebase Analytics. A local parent area protects subscription management; it does not verify parental consent.

Optional game analytics

Adults can separately turn on limited Firebase/Google Analytics events in Settings → Privacy. This is off by default. Events describe screens, completed rounds, chosen cosmetics, training outcomes and aggregate coin or challenge activity. We do not send precise movement, chat, names, birth dates or our local wallet identifier in these events. Google may process an analytics app-instance identifier, app and device details, timestamps and network information. No IDFA module, cross-app tracking or advertising personalization is enabled. Turning analytics off stops future events and resets the local analytics instance; previously collected aggregate reports may remain with Google under its retention settings.

Advertising

Ad requests are limited to adults after Google's consent process permits them. Advertising is configured as non-personalized with a General content rating. Google may still process IP address, device/app details, ad interactions and fraud-prevention information. Non-personalized does not mean no data processing. Ad privacy options are available in Settings when Google requires them. No advertising appears during a round, countdown, pause or training. Interstitials can appear between qualifying rounds with frequency limits. A rewarded video is optional; closing it alone never awards coins.

Verified video bonuses

If you request a video bonus, the app and our Firebase server use a random local profile identifier, a random Keychain installation secret, an opaque ticket and round identifier, App Check device-attestation information and Google's signed ad transaction to prevent duplicate rewards. The raw installation secret is not stored on the server. Reward records are separate from optional analytics and are not joined to your purchase identity. These services require internet access. Server logs may contain normal request metadata such as IP and timing; application logs are designed not to include tokens or raw reward URLs.

Subscriptions and RevenueCat

Apple processes payment; we do not receive your card details. RevenueCat verifies subscription status using Apple transaction and receipt information, a random RevenueCat app-user identifier, app/device information and network information. Adult purchase services can initialize independently of optional analytics to protect existing subscribers. A parent may also initialize purchase services to restore or manage an existing subscription for a protected age setting. We do not link RevenueCat identity to Firebase Analytics or send subscriber attributes. Restore Purchases restores an Apple subscription, not locally earned coins or cosmetic purchases.

Control, retention and requests

You can change age and analytics choices in Settings → Privacy. Apple's subscription settings control renewal. Local data remains until removed from the device; Keychain data can survive app deletion. Provider transaction records may be retained for fraud prevention, accounting or legal duties. We do not sell personal information. Service providers may process data outside your country under their applicable terms. To ask about access, deletion or privacy, contact malkiel1234@gmail.com. Do not send payment credentials or API keys. Anonymous data may require additional information to locate; we will not ask you to post it publicly.
