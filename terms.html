AirKickers Ad-Free · annual subscription

Ad-Free removes interstitial advertising and allows the equivalent optional round coin bonus without a rewarded video when the same eligibility and daily limits are met. It does not automatically unlock cosmetics or provide a separate coin pack. Protected age settings already receive ad-free play without buying a subscription.

Price and renewal

The selected US reference price is $2.99 per year. The App Store displays your localized price and applicable taxes before purchase. Payment is charged to your Apple account after confirmation. The subscription automatically renews for another year unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Apple may charge renewal within 24 hours before the end of the current period. There is no introductory trial in this version.

Manage, restore and cancel

You can manage or cancel renewal through your Apple account subscriptions. Canceling renewal generally keeps paid access through the end of the current period, subject to Apple's refund, revocation and billing rules. Use Restore Purchases in AirKickers to restore an existing eligible Apple subscription. Restore does not recover local scores, coins or cosmetic unlocks. Family Sharing is not promised.
